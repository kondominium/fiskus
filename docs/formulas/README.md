# Formulas

The income tax is worked out in steps, and each formula has its own page. They are listed in the order the calculation runs.

- [Basic allowance](grundfreibetrag.md): the zero zone, the amount in `rules/` that is not taxed.
- [Tariff](tariff.md): the zones of § 32a Abs. 1 EStG, and how the tax is rounded.
  - [Table steps](table-steps.md): the 36 € blocks of § 32a Abs. 2 EStG, which apply to VZ 2003.
- [Tariff coefficients](tariff-coefficients.md): the figures of every year, generated from `rules/`.
- [Solidarity surcharge](solidarity-surcharge.md): the surcharge of § 4 SolZG, charged on the income tax.
